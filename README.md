One line of awkward small talk that one robot says to another while
they ride an elevator together.

## Entry

> When it says 'doors closing', is that you talking, or is the elevator just better at small talk than both of us?

112 characters (limit: 1–200). The joke: the robot cannot tell the
elevator's own "doors closing" announcement apart from the other
robot speaking — and the turn is that the elevator itself is better
at small talk than both of them.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
